2. Information we collect

We collect information only when necessary to provide you a service. The types of data we may collect include:

  • Contact form: name, email address, phone number, and the message you send us.
  • QUELVO bookings: name, email, phone number, pickup and dropoff address, trip date and time, and service type.
  • JCDM Music Ride: session codes to join active sessions. No personal account or identifying data is required.
  • User account (if you register): email address and password stored securely using hashing.
  • Browsing data: IP address, browser type, pages visited, and visit duration, collected via Google Analytics 4.

3. How we use your information

We use your data to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and service requests.
  • Coordinate and confirm QUELVO transportation bookings.
  • Operate JCDM Music Ride sessions.
  • Send you communications related to the service you requested.
  • Improve our website and platforms through anonymous usage analytics.

We do not use your data for third-party advertising and we do not sell it to any company.

4. Third parties with access to your data

We work with the following external services that may process data on your behalf:

  • Google Analytics 4 — website traffic analytics. Data is anonymized. Google Privacy Policy ↗
  • WhatsApp / Meta — optional communication channel for service coordination. Only used if you choose to contact us this way.
  • YouTube Data API — music search in JCDM Music Ride. Searches are sent to Google's YouTube API. Google Privacy Policy ↗
  • Supabase — secure storage of user accounts, if you create an account on our site.
  • Railway / Vercel — hosting infrastructure. May log standard network request metadata.

5. Cookies

We use minimal, necessary cookies for site functionality (language preference, theme, user session). Google Analytics may set analytics cookies. We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

6. Data retention

Contact form and booking data is retained for as long as necessary to fulfill the requested service and for the period required by Florida tax law. Music Ride sessions are temporary and deleted automatically when they end. User accounts are retained while the account is active.

7. Security

We apply reasonable technical measures to protect your data: HTTPS on all communications, passwords stored with secure hashing, and restricted data access. No system is 100% infallible, but we take security seriously.

8. Your rights

You may contact us at any time to:

  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal obligations).

We will respond to your request within 30 business days.

9. Children

Our services are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe we have collected data from a minor, contact us and we will delete it.
